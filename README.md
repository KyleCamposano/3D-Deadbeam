Game Name: Deadbeam 3D

Group Members:
- Aden Moon - Level Design (build level, maze sections, lighting, and generator/exit placement) 
- Kyle Camposano - UI / Game Management (menus, HUD for health and battery and generator count, and game manager)
- Christopher Lopez - Player Design (first-person controller, camera, and flashlight)
- Amina Bendjennat - Enemy Design (enemy art, behavior, chasing, and freezing when hit by light)
- Whitney Anderson - Audio (ambient sounds, footsteps, and enemy/generator sound effects)

Current target for game: Create low visibility first person horror game. The player uses a flashlight that loses charge the more a player uses it. 
The player can find batteries scattered around the level to recharge a portion of the flashlight's battery. The player must find three generators 
hidden around the map to turn on the power and escape. However, the more generators that are turned on, the more enemies that will spawn and come after
the player making it more difficult to escape. The enemies can be stopped by using the flashlight on them. The enemies can do damage to the player's
health bar, and if the player takes enough damage, they will die and the game will be over. We plan to make the generators respawn in different places 
each play through.
